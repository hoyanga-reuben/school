Registration Guidance

  • Password Requirements:
    • Your password must not be too similar to your personal information.
    • It must contain at least 8 characters.
    • It cannot be a commonly used password.
    • It cannot be entirely numeric.
  • About the Teacher Transfer System:
    • This system allows teachers to request school transfers online without visiting the TAMISEMI office.
    • Choose your correct role: Teacher, School Officer, District Officer, or TAMISEMI Officer.
    • Each role has access to specific features and dashboards.
    • After registration, your account will be reviewed and activated by the system admin.
    • Teachers can request transfers, track progress, and receive updates via email.
    • Officers can manage, approve, or reject requests based on their level.
  • Tips:
    • Ensure your profile information is accurate and up-to-date.
    • Upload a professional profile picture if possible.
    • Use a working email – it will be used for activation and notifications.
